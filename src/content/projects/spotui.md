I have always been fasicanted by cool looking terminal ui's. Rather I saw them in movies, shows or inside some old magazines or images of the late '90 or early 2000. With the rise in popularity of the OpenTUI and one of the most popular examples being opencode, I really wanted to give it a try.

This is also a great oppertunity for me to learn something new. Designing an application like this is kind of similar to a desktop application. Nearly everything should and is happening on the backend and only exposed via a bridge, which the frontend can use.

EffectTS approach to this are (Atoms)[https://effect.website/docs/v4/api/effect/unstable/reactivity/Atom]. Yet unstable, but creat for reactivity. Most of this is new to me, learning a lot about system design and building proper interfaces.
